For My Jaanu

a little piece of my heart, just for you

Hi babe, kaisi ho? I hope aap bilkul theek ho.

Babe, pehle mujhe lagta tha ki sacha pyaar sirf filmon ki ek kahani hai. Jab bhi main sunta tha ki kisi ne pyaar mein sab kuch qurbaan kar diya, toh mujhe lagta tha ki mujhe itna chahne wala meri kismat mein kabhi nahi hoga. Main hamesha is darr mein rehta tha ki agar kisi ko mujhse pyaar ho bhi gaya, toh mujhse behtar koi milte hi woh mujhe chhod dega.

Par babe, aapne aakar sirf meri yeh soch hi nahi badli, balki meri poori hasti ko badal diya hai.

Sach kahun toh maine khwab mein bhi nahi socha tha ki aapke jaisi itni khoobsurat aur pyaari ladki mujhe kabhi apnayegi. Jab hum pehli baar mile the, tab bhi mujhe lagta tha ki aap meri pahunch se bahut door ho—aur sach toh yeh hai ki aap aaj bhi mere liye kisi farishte se kam nahi ho. Phir bhi aapne mujhe chuna, aur yeh mere liye meri poori duniya hai. Mujhse anjaane mein bahut galtiyan hui hain, par mera irada kabhi aapka dil dukhane ka nahi raha. Meri har saans sirf aapke saath rehne ki dua karti hai.

Aapke aane se meri andheri duniya mein ek noor sa aa gaya hai, babe. Mujhe har pal lagta hai ki sirf aapke liye aur aapki wajah se hi main zindagi mein kuch bada kar sakta hoon. Khud ke liye shayad main kuch na kar paun, par aapki khushi ke liye main poori duniya se lad sakta hoon. Main bas itna chahta hoon ki aapka pyaar mujh par hamesha bana rahe, aap mere saath hamesha muskurati raho, aur meri har chhoti-badi koshish par fakhr karo.

Babe, aap mere liye ek masoom, nanhi si jaan ho jise main har gham se bacha kar rakhna chahta hoon. Aap meri rooh ka sabse nazuk hissa ho; aapki muskurahat se meri saansein chalti hain, aur aapki aankhon mein ek aansu bhi aa jaye toh mera dam ghutne lagta hai. Aap mere jeene ki wajah ho, babe.

Main Allah se sirf 3 cheezein maangta hoon:

  1. Aapki hamesha ki khushi
  2. Aapki hifazat
  3. Har janam mein aapka saath

Isse zyada maine kabhi kuch nahi maanga, aur na hi kabhi Allah se maangunga 🤲🏻. Sirf yeh 3 cheezein mil jayein toh meri zindagi mukammal ho jayegi.

I love you endlessly, babe. Dil mein toh bahut kuch hai likhne ko, par aapke waqt ka khayal rakhte hue bas itna hi kahunga.

I love you, jaanu.

With all my love,Forever Yours, I Love You ♥
